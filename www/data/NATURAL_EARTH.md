# Globe land outline

`ne_110m_land.geojson` is Natural Earth 1:110m land geometry, obtained on
2026-10-24 from the Natural Earth vector repository:
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Natural Earth data are public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

It supplies a lightweight coastline/land background, not forecast data.
The globe rasterizes this vector geometry locally; no imagery or tile archive
is downloaded. Natural Earth is credited in the globe interface.
